HOW WE READ

Evaluating
sources

Attach a source to the particular fact it supports. Preserve the document name, date, population, unit and page or table. Say when a result is an estimate, a forecast, an opinion or an observation.

Authority is claim-specific. A company can know its operations and have an interest in presenting them favorably. A study can be methodical and still have limited coverage. More headlines do not necessarily mean more independent evidence.

FEAR AI.

We examine claims about AI through research, checking sources, methods and the scope of each finding. We distinguish measured effects from forecasts. Our essays offer interpretations and opinions alongside the research.
